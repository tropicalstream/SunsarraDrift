# SUNSARRA DRIFT — Story Bible

## Logline
Sunsarra Vex, a 19-year-old salvage pilot, chases the Confederate Empire across
the solar system and out to the galaxy's heart to rescue her mother — the
geneticist whose data proves the Empire's eugenics creed is a lie. She wins
not by burning the Empire down, but by opening it: broadcasting the truth from
the edge of a black hole.

## Literary spine
- **Ursula K. Le Guin** — the Empire is an "ambiguous utopia" gone rotten:
  clean, orderly, walled. "Walls are made of ideas, and ideas of walls" drives
  Act VI's liberation of the Variance convoy. Victory = unbuilding a wall.
- **Carl Sagan** — the finale is a pale-blue-dot address (Dr. Obi, m12_out4);
  science as a candle held against a demon-haunted creed.
- **Isaac Asimov** — the long game: violence buys minutes, *ideas* end empires
  (the broadcast is a Foundation-style victory-by-inevitability). Tern, the
  ship AI, carries the robot-ethics thread: a machine gentler than the men
  who claim to perfect humanity.
- **Arthur C. Clarke** — the Silent Cylinder (mission 9) is the Rama beat: an
  artifact two billion years old, gloriously unoptimized, that endured by
  *changing*. Wonder as counter-argument.

## The moral engine (anti-eugenics, done with real science)
Dr. Adaeze Obi and Dr. Maren Vex argue from the actual literature:
- **Stephen Jay Gould**, *The Mismeasure of Man* — ranking humans on a single
  scale is a category error built on biased instruments (m06).
- **Gould's contingency** ("replay the tape of life") — evolution has no
  ladder, no "Optimal" endpoint (m09).
- **Richard Lewontin (1972)** — ~85% of human genetic variation lies *within*
  populations; the Empire's castes are statistical fiction (m06_out1, m10).
- **Cavalli-Sforza** — humanity is one braided river of ancestry (m10_out2).
- **Monoculture fragility** — cloned doctrine loses to diverse crews on
  screen, in gameplay (m07, m11): the theme is *playable*, not preached.
Character arc: rage → mercy (sparing the Empire pilot, m05) → purpose
(publish, don't punish, m06) → command (m11) → the open cage (m12).
